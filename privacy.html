Privacy Policy — Kind Hangul Viewer

Effective: November 9, 2026

Kind Hangul Viewer is a free document viewer developed by MINSUK SONG. There are no advertisements, accounts, analytics SDKs or in-app purchases.

Documents and signatures

The app processes HWP and HWPX documents locally on your device. It does not upload documents, document text, drawings or signatures to our servers. We do not operate a document-processing server. A copy of opened files is kept in the app’s private storage for the recent-document list. Saved signatures and preferences are also stored locally. Device backups may include app data according to your operating system and backup settings.

Your choices

Removing an item from Recent documents deletes only its local app copy. Removing a saved signature deletes the app’s saved signature. Your source files and previously exported files are unchanged. Uninstalling the app removes its local data according to your operating system. Copies you export to Files or another app are managed by that destination.

File and photo access

The app uses the system file and photo picker to access only items you choose. It does not require access to all photos or contacts. Signing with your finger requires no camera access. You choose where to save or share an exported document.

Reviews and external services

The app can request a review through Apple or Google’s official review interface and open the app’s store listing. The store provider controls its interface and handles any review you submit. The app keeps only local usage counts and request dates to avoid frequent prompts; it cannot see your rating or review completion.

Support

If you email lalajoo71@gmail.com, we use your email and message to respond. Please avoid sending private documents or signature images. No support message is sent automatically.

Children and changes

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. The effective date will be updated here.
